Mai Tai

Trader vics style Mai Tai - the king of Tiki cocktails.

Ingredients

  • 60 ml Havana Club Añejo 7 Años Cuban Rum
  • 15 ml Ferrand Dry Curaçao liqueur
  • 22.5 ml Lime juice (freshly squeezed)
  • 10 ml Monin Orgeat syrup
  • 7.5 ml Monin Pure Cane sugar syrup (Equivalent to 2 sugar to 1 water simple syrup)

Steps

  1. Chill a tiki or Double Old fashioned glass
  2. Prepare a small fruit stick with a chunk of pineapple and a glace cherry
  3. Cut quarter slice of lime
  4. Prepare Sprig of Lime
  5. Shake the ingredients with ice
  6. Add crushed ice to glass
  7. Strain into the glass
  8. Garnish with fruit stick, mint and lime.
